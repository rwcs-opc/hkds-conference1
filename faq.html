Frequently Asked Questions

The Institute for Sustainable Technology and Societal Development (ISTSD) is a multidisciplinary research and innovation institute. We specialize in researching and creating sustainable technologies, renewable energy system setups, smart community solutions, and AI algorithms built for environmental and social good.
Our core focus spans Sustainable Agriculture (IoT & sensors), Renewable Energy (smart local grids), AI & Data Science for Social Good, Smart Communities, Digital Infrastructure Transformation, and Educational Capacity Building.
Yes, ISTSD maintains structured partnerships with academic institutions, engineering departments, corporate technology labs, and non-governmental development organizations. These alliances enable us to bridge advanced laboratory research with real-world community field testing.
We welcome applications from research fellows, PhD scholars, and field scientists who are passionate about sustainable technology systems. Contact our team via our form specifying your research background.
Yes, we conduct hands-on training workshops, educational seminars, and research symposiums on topics such as AI for Sustainable Development, IoT precision farming systems, and smart microgrid maintenance. Details are listed under our Events section.
Organizations interested in joint research, pilot implementations, technology licensing, or institutional funding can register a query through our contact form. Our partnerships division will schedule a consultation call.
We offer structured internship programs, coding bootcamps, and master's thesis mentorships. Active opportunities are posted periodically. Interested students can contact us with a summary of their research interests.
